The library in a `MudDrawer`, as one toolbar over two columns: the channels on the left and the selected channel's recordings to their right. Search, sort and filter live here, with the results.

## Build it from

- **Toolbar:** a grid with three rows. Row one: `MudText Typo.h6` "Library", the current order as the `MudMenu` trigger itself (`Newest first` + `KeyboardArrowDown`; no separate `MoreVert`), and a `Close` icon button. Row two: the count line in `caption` ("4 channels, 8 recordings") spanning the full width. Row three: `SearchField` full width, then the `MudChipSet`.
- **Sort:** `MudMenu` with `ActivatorContent`; the activator content is an inert `span.sk-sort` with `data-sort` and `@onclick="menu.ToggleAsync"`. The wrapper MudMenu renders is already `role="button"`; a nested `<button>` would be a nested interactive control. Newest first, Oldest first, A–Z by channel; the current item has `aria-current="true"`.
- **Search:** the only search field in the app. Leading `Search` icon, 36px high on `surface`, placeholder "Search library", `aria-keyshortcuts="/"`, a `/` keycap on fine pointers (hidden on coarse), and a 250ms debounce that writes `?q=`.
- **Filters:** `MudChipSet` single-select, `CheckMark="false"`, All (`ViewList`), Recording (`FiberManualRecord` in `rec`) and Saved (`Inventory2`); the selected chip is filled with `primary`. Below 360px viewport width the chip icons are hidden so the three labels still fit.
- **Paused note:** `MudAlert Severity.Warning Dense` "Live updates paused". app CSS shows it while the circuit reconnects (`body[data-sk-reconnect]`).
- **Channels column** (`drawer-width`, 320px): a single-select list (`role="listbox"`, options `role="option"` with `aria-selected`). Each row is a full-width button (56px): a `ChannelAvatar` (the channel's Twitch image or its initial on its channel colour; the `rec` badge while capturing), the name in `subtitle1`, the meta line in `caption` ("Recording now", "Paused", or the day of the latest recording; recording meta is `text-primary`), the count in `caption` at the right, and a `ChevronRight` that is visible on hover/focus/selection on hover-capable devices. The selected channel is on `selection` with a 3px `primary` bar at its left edge and every line in `text-primary`.
- **Recordings column** (`rec-col-width`: `clamp(360px, 28vw, 440px)`), to the right of the channels:
  - **Header** (56px): the channel's name in `h6`, its meta in `caption` ("Recording now · 3 recordings"), and a `ChevronLeft` button ("Close nightjar_tv's recordings") that collapses the column. The duplicate avatar is dropped on desktop and kept on compact.
  - **Body**, scrolling on its own: sticky day headers in `label` type on `drawer-background`, then `RecordingRow`s, newest first. The current recording keeps `aria-current`.
  - **Deleting:** each row has its own Delete button (see `RecordingRow`), and Delete on a focused row does the same. Both open the `Dialog` "Delete recording?" over the page, with the drawer left open behind it. Escape in the dialog closes only the dialog. On confirm the row collapses out (150ms, none under reduced motion), the channel's count and the column header's count drop by one, focus moves to the next row (or the previous one, or the column's Close button when it was the last), and a snackbar says "Recording deleted". Deleting the watched recording does what deleting it from the title block does. When a channel's last recording goes, the channel leaves the list and the column closes.
  - It opens with a 200ms slide from under the channels (no motion under reduced motion); switching channels swaps its contents without closing it. The column casts the overlay's right-edge shadow only; the pane boundary is a hairline.
- **Disk usage footer** (`StorageMeter`), pinned to the bottom of the channels, above a `divider`: the `Storage` icon, "1.2 TB free" in `caption` at Medium with "of 4 TB" right-aligned, and a full-width `MudProgressLinear Size.Small` of the space used, in `primary`. Under 10% free, the icon becomes `Warning`, and the icon, the free figure and the bar turn `warning`. The tooltip gives "Recording disk: 1.2 TB free of 4 TB". It renders nothing when the size is unknown.
- **Mini rail** (`DrawerVariant.Mini`, 72px): no toolbar, sort or filters. A `Home` icon button, a short divider, then channel avatars at 32px, the current streamer ringed in `primary`, the name in a `MudTooltip`. A capturing channel carries a 14px `rec` badge, ringed 2px in `drawer-background`, pulsing to 35% opacity and back every 1.6s (still under `prefers-reduced-motion`). At the bottom, a `Storage` icon button (`Warning` under 10%) with the same tooltip and a 24×3px used-disk gauge beneath it.

## The consumer provides

`ILibraryView` (streamers, recordings, `IsLoaded`), the current query (sort, state, search), the selected recording id, the selected channel, and the viewer's time zone for day headers.

## Rules

- A channel that is recording sorts to the top.
- **Which channel is selected.** Every time it opens: while watching, on the current recording's channel, with that recording's row scrolled into view; elsewhere (Home, Settings), with nothing selected, as the channels column alone. A channel picked in the open drawer is forgotten when it closes. Clicking a rail avatar is the one way to open it on another channel.
- **Width.** The channels column is 320px; the recordings column is `clamp(360px, 28vw, 440px)`. On desktop the library opens over the clipped main area (no overlay), so the page never reflows.
- **Search and filters** apply to both columns. A channel with no matching recordings drops out; counts show matches, not totals. If the selected channel drops out, its column closes.
- **Opening and closing.** The library opens from the app bar toggle, the search shortcut, a rail avatar, the storage button, Home's "Show all", or the tally. It closes on the close button, Escape, a press outside on wide, a path change, or selecting a row on compact. A query-only change (search, filter, sort) keeps it open at every width, so the search field stays usable on a phone.
- **Focus.** Opening via Library focuses the selected channel, else the first channel. Opening via Search (or `/`) focuses the search field after the open. Closing returns focus to the opener. Below Md the temporary drawer traps focus; wide it does not.
- **Keyboard** (replaces `tree-nav.js`): ↑ ↓ move through channels, Home and End jump to the first and last; Enter, Space or → select the channel, open its column and move focus to its first recording; in the column, ↑ ↓ move through recordings and ← or Escape go back to the selected channel. Escape on the channels closes the drawer.
- **Below `Breakpoint.Md`** there is no room for two columns, so the library drills in: tapping a channel slides its recordings over the channels list, with an `ArrowBack` button ("All channels") to return. The toolbar stays above the drill-in.
- While loading, show six skeleton channel rows (`MudSkeleton`), not a spinner. A recordings column never loads on its own: it renders from the library already in memory. With no channels: the `VideoLibrary` empty state, "No recordings yet", and a link to Settings › Channels. With no match: "No recordings match" and its one way out.
- Selecting a row in the temporary drawer closes the drawer.
