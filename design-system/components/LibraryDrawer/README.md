The library in a `MudDrawer`, as two columns: the channels on the left, and the selected channel's recordings in a second column to their right. Newest first at every level, filtered by chips and by the app-bar search.

## Build it from

- **Header:** `MudText Typo.h6` "Library", the order in `caption` ("Newest first"), and a `MudMenu` (`Icons.Material.Rounded.MoreVert`, `Size.Small`) with Newest first, Oldest first and A–Z by streamer as checkable items.
- **Filters:** `<MudChipSet T="string" SelectionMode="SelectionMode.SingleSelection" SelectedColor="Color.Primary" Variant="Variant.Outlined" CheckMark>` with All (`ViewList`), Recording (`FiberManualRecord` in `rec`) and Saved (`Inventory2`). The icon gives way to the check mark when a chip is selected.
- **Paused note:** `MudAlert Severity.Warning Dense` "Live updates paused". app CSS shows it while the circuit reconnects (`body[data-sk-reconnect]`).
- **Channels column** (`drawer-width`, 320px): a single-select list (`role="listbox"`, the rows `role="option"` with `aria-selected`) replacing the old tree. Each row is a full-width button: a `ChannelAvatar` (`Size.Large`, the channel's Twitch image or its initial on its channel colour; the `rec` badge while capturing), the name in `subtitle1`, the meta line in `caption` ("Recording now", or the day of the latest recording), the recording count in `caption` at the right, and a `ChevronRight`. The selected channel is on `selection` with a 3px `primary` bar at its left edge, every line in `text-primary`. Rows do not expand any more.
- **Recordings column** (360px), opened to the right of the channels when a channel is selected, inside the same drawer and on `drawer-background`, divided from the channels by a `divider` rule:
  - **Header** (64px): the channel's avatar, its name in `h6`, its meta in `caption` ("Recording now · 3 recordings"), and a `Close` icon button ("Close nightjar_tv's recordings") that closes the column and leaves the channels.
  - **Body**, scrolling on its own: day headers in `label` type, then `RecordingRow`s, newest first. The current recording keeps `aria-current`.
  - **Deleting:** each row has its own Delete button (see `RecordingRow`), and Delete on a focused row does the same. Both open the `Dialog` "Delete recording?" over the page, with the drawer left open behind it. On confirm the row collapses out (150 ms, none under reduced motion), the channel's count and the column header's count drop by one, focus moves to the next row (or the previous one, or the column's Close button when it was the last), and a snackbar says "Recording deleted". Deleting the recording you are watching does what deleting it from the title block does. When a channel's last recording goes, the channel leaves the list and the column closes.
  - It opens with a 200 ms slide from under the channels (no motion under reduced motion); switching channels swaps its contents without closing it.
- **Disk usage footer** (`StorageMeter`), pinned to the bottom of the drawer below the scrolling channels, above a `divider` rule: the `Storage` icon, "1.2 TB free" in `caption` at Medium with "of 4 TB" right-aligned, and a full-width `MudProgressLinear Size.Small` of the space used, in `primary`. Under 10% free, the icon becomes `Warning`, and the icon, the free figure and the bar turn `warning`. The tooltip gives "Recording disk: 1.2 TB free of 4 TB". It renders nothing when the size is unknown.
- **Mini rail** (`DrawerVariant.Mini`, 72px; the default while watching on desktop): a `Home` icon button, a short divider, then streamer avatars at 32px (`MudAvatar Size.Medium`, smaller than the open drawer's 40px), the current streamer ringed in `primary`, the name in a `MudTooltip`. A streamer being captured carries the app bar logo's live dot, larger: a 14px `rec` disc at the avatar's top-right corner, ringed 2px in `drawer-background`, pulsing to 35% opacity and back every 1.6 s like the logo's dot (still under `prefers-reduced-motion`). The open drawer keeps its own avatars and badge. Clicking a rail avatar opens the drawer with that channel already selected, its recordings column open. At the bottom, the footer shrinks to a `Storage` icon button (`Warning` in `warning` under 10% free) with the same tooltip.

## The consumer provides

`ILibraryView` (streamers, recordings, `IsLoaded`), the current query (sort, state, search), the selected recording id, the selected channel, and the viewer's time zone for day headers.

## Rules

- A channel that is recording sorts to the top.
- **Which channel is selected.** While watching, the drawer opens on the current recording's channel. Elsewhere (Home, Settings) it opens with nothing selected, as the channels column alone; the last channel picked is remembered for the session only, not in `UiPreferences` (the old collapsed-groups slot goes away).
- **Width.** Both columns are 680px together. On desktop the drawer opens over the clipped main area (`DrawerVariant.Temporary` over the rail, no overlay), so the page never reflows; with only the channels column it stays 320px.
- **Search and filters** apply to both columns. A channel with no matching recordings drops out of the list; the count shows matches, not the total. If the selected channel drops out, its column closes.
- **Keyboard** (replaces `tree-nav.js`): ↑ ↓ move through channels, Home and End jump to the first and last; Enter, Space or → select the channel, open its column and move focus to its first recording; in the column, ↑ ↓ move through recordings and ← or Escape go back to the selected channel. Escape on the channels closes the drawer.
- **Below `Breakpoint.Md`** there is no room for two columns, so the library drills in: tapping a channel slides its recordings over the channels list in the same drawer, with an `ArrowBack` button in the header ("All channels") to return.
- While loading, show six skeleton channel rows (`MudSkeleton`), not a spinner; a column that is still fetching shows three skeleton recording rows. With no results: "No recordings match" when filtered, otherwise "No recordings yet".
- Selecting a row in the temporary drawer closes the drawer.
