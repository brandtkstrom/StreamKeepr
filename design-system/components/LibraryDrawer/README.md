The library in a `MudDrawer`, as one sheet: a toolbar over the 320px channel index on the left and the selected channel's recordings pane to its right. The toolbar spans the sheet, so search, sort and filter visibly belong to both panes; the pane is the raised content ground, and the index is navigation.

## Build it from

- **Toolbar:** a grid that spans the whole sheet. The heading block: `MudText Typo.h6` "Library" with the count line in `caption` ("4 channels, 8 recordings") under it, the current order as the `MudMenu` trigger itself (`Newest first` + `KeyboardArrowDown`; no separate `MoreVert`), and a `Close` icon button. Below it, `SearchField` on the left and the `MudChipSet` on the right share one row on the wide sheet. Narrower (a 320px sheet with no pane, and compact), the search and chips stack.
- **Sort:** `MudMenu` with `ActivatorContent`; the activator content is an inert `span.sk-sort` with `data-sort` and `@onclick="menu.ToggleAsync"`. The wrapper MudMenu renders is already `role="button"`; a nested `<button>` would be a nested interactive control. Newest first, Oldest first, A–Z by channel; the current item has `aria-current="true"`.
- **Search:** the only search field in the app. Leading `Search` icon, 36px high on `surface`, placeholder "Search library", `aria-keyshortcuts="/"`, a `/` keycap on fine pointers (hidden on coarse), and a 250ms debounce that writes `?q=`.
- **Filters:** `MudChipSet` single-select, `CheckMark="false"`, All (`ViewList`), Recording (`FiberManualRecord` in `rec`) and Saved (`Inventory2`); the selected chip is filled with `primary`. Below 360px viewport width the chip icons are hidden so the three labels still fit.
- **Paused note:** `MudAlert Severity.Warning Dense` "Live updates paused". app CSS shows it while the circuit reconnects (`body[data-sk-reconnect]`).
- **Channels column** (`drawer-width`, 320px): a single-select list (`role="listbox"`, options `role="option"` with `aria-selected`). Each row is a full-width button (56px): a `ChannelAvatar` (the channel's Twitch image or its initial on its channel colour; the `rec` badge while capturing), the name in `subtitle1`, the meta line in `caption` ("Recording now", "Paused", or the day of the latest recording; recording meta is `text-primary`), the matching-recording count as a quiet pill in `caption` at the right, and a `ChevronRight` that is visible on hover/focus/selection on hover-capable devices. The selected channel is on `selection` with a 3px `primary` bar at its left edge and every line in `text-primary`.
- **Recordings pane** (`rec-col-width`: `clamp(360px, 28vw, 440px)`), the right-hand half of the sheet on `surface`:
  - **Before a pick (wide).** A centered prompt on the pane ground: a 48px `VideoLibrary` icon in `action-default`, "Choose a channel" in `subtitle1`, and "Its recordings open here, ready to watch or delete." in `body2`. Not focusable; the first pick replaces it.
  - **Header** (56px): a back control at the seam (left) — `ChevronLeft` "Close nightjar_tv's recordings" wide, `ArrowBack` "All channels" compact — then the channel's 40px avatar (kept at every width; it is the pane's channel identity), the name in `h6`, and its meta in `caption` ("Recording now · 3 recordings"). One control, at the side the pane came from; there is no second close at the far right.
  - **Body**, scrolling on its own: sticky day headers in `label` type on `surface`, then `RecordingRow`s, newest first. The current recording keeps `aria-current`.
  - **Deleting:** each row has its own Delete button (see `RecordingRow`), and Delete on a focused row does the same. Both open the `Dialog` "Delete recording?" over the page, with the drawer left open behind it. Escape in the dialog closes only the dialog. On confirm the row collapses out (150ms, none under reduced motion), the channel's count and the pane header's count drop by one, focus moves to the next row (or the previous one, or the pane's back control when it was the last), and a snackbar says "Recording deleted". Deleting the watched recording does what deleting it from the title block does. When a channel's last recording goes, the channel leaves the list and the pane closes.
  - It opens with a 200ms slide from under the channels (no motion under reduced motion); switching channels swaps its contents without closing it. The whole sheet casts one shadow; the pane boundary is a hairline, not a second floating layer.
- **Disk usage footer** (`StorageMeter`), pinned to the bottom of the channel index, above a `divider`: the `Storage` icon, "1.2 TB free" in `caption` at Medium with "of 4 TB" right-aligned, and a full-width `MudProgressLinear Size.Small` of the space used, in `primary`. Under 10% free, the icon becomes `Warning`, and the icon, the free figure and the bar turn `warning`. The tooltip gives "Recording disk: 1.2 TB free of 4 TB". It renders nothing when the size is unknown. The pane continues to the sheet's bottom beside it.
- **Mini rail** (`DrawerVariant.Mini`, 72px): no toolbar, sort or filters. A `Home` icon button, a short divider, then channel avatars at 32px, the current streamer ringed in `primary`, the name in a `MudTooltip`. A capturing channel carries a 14px `rec` badge, ringed 2px in `drawer-background`, pulsing to 35% opacity and back every 1.6s (still under `prefers-reduced-motion`). At the bottom, a `Storage` icon button (`Warning` under 10%) with the same tooltip and a 24×3px used-disk gauge beneath it.

## The consumer provides

`ILibraryView` (streamers, recordings, `IsLoaded`), the current query (sort, state, search), the selected recording id, the selected channel, and the viewer's time zone for day headers.

## Rules

- A channel that is recording sorts to the top.
- **Which channel is selected.** Every time it opens: while watching, on the current recording's channel, with that recording's row scrolled into view; elsewhere (Home, Settings), with nothing selected, as the 320px channel index plus the wide "Choose a channel" prompt. A channel picked in the open drawer is forgotten when it closes. Clicking a rail avatar is the one way to open it on another channel; it clears any retained search/state first, because the rail is not filtered.
- **Width.** One sheet: the 320px channel index plus the `clamp(360px, 28vw, 440px)` recordings pane. It widens to that full width only when a pane shows; with no channels, or no search match, it stays 320px. On desktop the library opens over the clipped main area (no overlay), so the page never reflows.
- **Search and filters** apply to both panes. A channel with no matching recordings drops out; counts show matches, not totals. If the selected channel drops out, its pane closes; with no matching channels the sheet returns to 320px and shows the no-match state. The closed rail ignores the search and state filter and always shows every channel; the chosen sort still orders it.
- **Opening and closing.** The library opens from the app bar toggle, the search shortcut, a rail avatar, the storage button, Home's "Show all", or the tally. It closes on the close button, Escape, a press outside on wide, a path change, or selecting a row on compact. A query-only change (search, filter, sort) keeps it open at every width, so the search field stays usable on a phone.
- **Focus.** Opening via Library focuses the selected channel, else the first channel. Opening via Search (or `/`) focuses the search field after the open. Closing returns focus to the opener. Below Md the temporary drawer traps focus; wide it does not.
- **Keyboard** (replaces `tree-nav.js`): ↑ ↓ move through channels, Home and End jump to the first and last; Enter, Space or → select the channel, open its pane and move focus to its first recording; in the pane, ↑ ↓ move through recordings and ← or Escape go back to the selected channel. Escape on the channels closes the drawer.
- **Below `Breakpoint.Md`** there is no room for two panes, so the library drills in: tapping a channel slides its recordings over the channel index, with an `ArrowBack` button ("All channels") to return. The toolbar stays above the drill-in, so search, filter and sort stay in reach.
- While loading, show six skeleton channel rows (`MudSkeleton`), not a spinner; wide, the pane mirrors them with a skeleton header and three rows. A recordings pane never loads on its own: it renders from the library already in memory. With no channels: the `VideoLibrary` empty state, "No recordings yet", and a link to Settings › Channels. With no match: "No recordings match" and its one way out. Neither state appears in the rail.
- Selecting a row in the temporary drawer closes the drawer.
